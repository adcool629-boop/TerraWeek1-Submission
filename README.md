# TerraWeek1 Submission
This is my submission for Terra Week 1

--------------------
A backstory on the game

I made this game because I wanted to capture essence of adventure
I also wanted to fufill the theme of outdoors that was offered to me within the Terra Community.
I hope you enjoy my game, and look forward to my next work.

--------------------
The Premise

It is a 2d Top Down game in which you must roam the world within the borders and get to the end
You must find your way through enemies and 3 different scenes.
Complete the plotline and defeat the enemies.
--------------------
The Objectives


Find the Cave
Navigate your way through the darkness
Find the Last Message
Instruction on inputs are in the game.
--------------------
The Plot

You are an adventurer who has recieved a request to save a village from a constant attack of monsters.
 You must discern the right way to go.
Then you must  Naviagate through the cave in darkness and reach the village.

--------------------
The inputs

W to move Up
S to move Down
A to move left
D to move right
Space Bar to attack

--------------------
Items
You can collect potions that give different effects to your character

--------------------
UI

There is a health bar in the top left make sure to keep an eye on it
There is an objective in the top right screen to help you progress through the game
On notes there is a button at the bottom to close
